# TechX Systems: company brief

Source: "TechX Systems Company Research.pdf" (prepared 14 October 2026). It drew on the live site's 16 pages and 94 images, Internet Archive copies from 2013 to 2025, the domain record and a web search.

## What they do

A small photo-editing company in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh. It edits property photos for real estate photographers and agents, mostly in the USA and Australia, at very low prices (from $0.70 a photo).

## How they work

Clients upload raw photos and send instructions by email, Skype or WhatsApp. Edited photos come back in 12–24 hours. New clients get a free trial. There's no online checkout: every "Buy" button leads to the contact form.

## Services and prices

| Service | Price | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Photo editing / retouching | $0.70–1.50 per image | Lens correction, HDR, colour correction, sky and grass replacement, fire in fireplaces, TV screen fill, straightening |

Portfolio counts: Interior 33, Exterior 18, Drone 15, Yacht 10, Twilight 8, Floor plan 3. It has no before/after comparisons. Virtual staging, 3D tours and app development were offered in 2018 and later dropped.

## Brand assets

- **Taglines:** "The Post-Production Company", "You capture the media, we do the rest" (kept as the main message), "Exceptional Starts Here", "Your Photos need a Champion Touching".
- **Logo:** a blue camera outline under a maroon house roof, with a shutter inside the house. The wordmark is "techX systems" with a red brush-style X.
- **Colours and fonts:** blue, maroon and grey; Montserrat and Open Sans. The new system keeps the blue and maroon and replaces both fonts with Geist.

## USPs

- **Now:** low prices, free trial, 24/7 support, 12–24 hour delivery.
- **2018, since removed:** unlimited revisions, no advance payment, no minimum order, 100% money-back guarantee. The new design brings back free revisions and pay-after-delivery, pending client confirmation.

## Weak spots of the current site

- Frozen since 2019, with no HTTPS ("Not secure" warning).
- Empty blog, and a broken "CEO Message" link.
- Spelling mistakes: "Corection", "Twillight", "indivisuals".
- The 2016 site had text copied from Flatworld Solutions.
- No social links, outside reviews or sitemap.
- Inconsistent numbers: the 2018 site said 12 countries, 42 clients and 1,650 projects; today it says 4 countries, 20 clients and 47,230 "projects", which are probably images.
- The "London office" is very likely a virtual-office address.
