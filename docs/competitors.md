# Competitors: Fotello and Imagen

Both homepages were captured in the Figma file "Freight Options" (`EnYy34jk7jKka2LrSlrRIv`). Fotello's pricing came from its live pricing page. Imagen blocks automated access, so its details come from the Figma capture and search results. Researched October 2026.

## Fotello (fotello.co, Vancouver)

An AI platform for real estate photographers that covers booking, editing, delivery and payment. It claims to market "1 in 4 homes worldwide" and shows "4.9 across 1,000+ reviews".

**Look:** white and airy, with soft pink and lilac gradients, rounded cards and a geometric sans-serif. CTAs are rose/pink. The page is very long.

**Homepage order:**
1. Announcement bar (Partner plan)
2. Nav: Products ▾, Pricing, phone, Sign in, Book a demo, "Start for FREE"
3. Hero: "The AI real estate media platform, built to grow your business"; star rating; workflow tabs (Rank, Book, Edit & Personalize, Deliver, Upsell)
4. Mock photographer website ("Real estate media, delivered by noon")
5. Logo marquee
6. Video testimonials
7. "One shoot. Complete Listing Media": tabbed showcase for Photos, Staging and Twilight, crediting the photographer and camera
8. Feature grid: Get more orders · Delivery that builds your brand · Get paid on your terms · Schedule efficiently · Simplify communication · Realtor collaboration · AutoUpsell
9. Trust row: unlimited storage, 24/7 support, MLS compliant
10. Closing CTA and a footer listing every product

**Pricing:**
- Billing toggle: pay as you go / monthly (−40%) / yearly (−60%)
- Free: $0, up to 30 photos with branding
- Essential: $16 per listing (75 listings, $1,200/month)
- Ultimate: $18 per listing ($1,350/month, "Most popular")
- Partner and Enterprise: custom
- Pay-as-you-go: editing $0.50/photo; twilight and staging $1 each; video from $28
- A savings calculator (photos per listing × price per photo × twilights) and a plan comparison table

**Borrowed:** the tabbed before/after showcase, photographer and camera credits, the savings calculator, MLS-compliance reassurance, a footer that lists every service.

**Left out:** booking, scheduling and payment software (TechX doesn't sell these), the 13-item product menu, the page length.

## Imagen (imagen-ai.com/real-estate)

AI editing software with a real estate product. It runs pages aimed directly at outsourced editors like TechX, e.g. "Replace real estate photo editing companies" and "The last editing company you'll hire".

**Look:** near-black hero with a single lime accent, a calm technical tone and lots of whitespace.

**Homepage order:**
1. Early-bird bar ("30% off – $0.22/photo")
2. Nav: How it works, Features, Pricing, Q&A
3. Hero: "From shoot to listing, without the wait", with a product UI
4. Testimonial
5. "Upload. Process. Deliver." in 3 steps ("No installation required")
6. Feature video
7. Pricing: pay-as-you-go at $0.22/photo (normally $0.32) and custom pricing for teams editing 25,000+ photos a year
8. Draggable before/after slider ("Built for teams that can't afford inconsistency")
9. Six features
10. Testimonials
11. "Processing 2,000+ images a month?"
12. Closing CTA

The $0.22 price was only seen on a test version of their site, so check it before quoting. Their free trial is 250 photos.

**Borrowed:** the 3-step flow, the draggable slider, two clear price options (pay-as-you-go and teams), a high-volume studio section.

**Left out:** competing on price per photo (AI will always be cheaper), and the dark software look, which reads as an app rather than a team.

## Where TechX wins

1. **A person checks every photo.** Hand work on fire, TV screens, window pulls and sky swaps, with no AI artefacts.
2. **One team for everything.** Photos, twilight, staging, drone, floor plans and video, on one invoice.
3. **Edited overnight.** India works while the USA and Australia sleep.
4. **Real people to talk to.** WhatsApp, email or a call, and a saved style guide per client.
