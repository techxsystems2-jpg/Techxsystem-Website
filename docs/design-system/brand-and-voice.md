# Brand and voice

## Strategy (checklist step 1)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | Free-trial sign-ups. One action on every page: send us 10 photos. |
| **Audience** | Real estate photographers and studio owners in the USA and Australia (also the UK and Canada). Visual, busy, often at a laptop after a shoot day. They judge everything by the pictures. |
| **Platform** | Webflow suggested (visual CMS so TechX can add portfolio pairs and posts). To confirm. |
| **Personality** | Precise · Calm · Craft-led · Quietly premium · Human |
| **Positioning** | A trained team, not an app. A person checks every photo, it's edited overnight, and one team handles every deliverable. |

## Main message

> **You capture the media. We do the rest.**

TechX's own tagline. It leads the Home hero and the final call to action, and must survive every redesign.

Supporting lines, used consistently:
- Real estate photo editing by people, not presets.
- Upload after the shoot; ready by morning, US time.
- From $0.70 an image.
- A person checks every photo.
- Ten photos free. No card, no contract.

## Voice

Write like a senior editor talking to a photographer: plain, specific, unhurried.

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Back by morning, US time. | Revolutionary AI-powered solutions |
| A person checks every photo. | World-class quality guaranteed!! |
| From $0.70 an image. | Unlock your potential |
| Talk to an editor, not a ticket. | Contact us for pricing |

Rules:
- Name things the way photographers do: brackets, window pull, verticals, twilight, MLS.
- Show the price. Never hide cost behind "contact us".
- Use active voice. Buttons say exactly what happens: "Start free trial", "Send my free trial", "Download all".
- No invented proof. Testimonials, logos and numbers stay as marked placeholders until they're real.
- Fix the old spelling errors ("Corection", "Twillight").

## Headline pattern

Two-tone headlines carry most sections: a short lead statement in ink, then the explanation in muted grey.

> **Five answers, up front.** <span style="color:#9AA1AD">How we work, what it costs, how to start and who to call.</span>
