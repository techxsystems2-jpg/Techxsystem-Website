## Home: "Five answers, up front"

Right after the hero slider and the logo strip, Home has a bento of five answer cards, each with its own illustration and link:

1. How we do it → How it works

4. How to start → Free trial

## Rules

1. Every page ends with the free-trial call to action and the contact block.
2. Every service card shows its starting price. No page hides cost behind "Contact us".
3. On mobile, the sticky bar keeps "Start trial" on screen after the hero scrolls away.
4. The main message, "You capture the media. We do the rest.", opens Home and closes the final CTA.
5. Any future redesign must pass this table before it ships.
