## Navigation

- **Desktop nav:** Services · Work · How it works · Pricing · Contact + Log in + **Start free trial**
- **Mobile nav:** Logo · **Free trial** · menu, plus a sticky bottom bar ("10 photos free · Start trial")
- **Footer:** every service, plus Company, Resources and Start

About and Resources live in the footer, which keeps the main nav to five items (per the checklist: 5–7).
